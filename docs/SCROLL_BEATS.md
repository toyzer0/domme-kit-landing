- **Line:** Look at me first. Don’t rush the words.
- **Plate → eyes:** Full-bleed face or chest press. Hold center. Soft blink / slow sway — **stare lock**, not montage. Eyes stay on her until the scroll hand twitches.

## Between-section drip (glue)
Between every `---` on the product page, one breath-line max (Eliza second-person):
- “Keep scrolling… slower…”
- “That’s it… empty a little more…”
- “Don’t think the next part. Feel it.”
Never stack two drips. Never Soft-49. Pic always before the next drip.
